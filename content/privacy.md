Paravida is designed for research and game design exploration. The app does not require users to create an account for regular play.

If you participate in a research study, we may collect study responses, optional contact information, and app interaction data needed to understand the design task. Research data is handled according to the consent information provided for that study.

We do not sell personal information. We only use collected information to operate the app, support the research process, improve the design experience, and respond to participant or media enquiries.

For privacy questions or removal requests, contact the Paravida team using the contact page. Replace this placeholder policy with your reviewed legal text before App Store submission.
