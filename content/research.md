Participants will install Paravida, play a short set of included games, then use the design panel to adjust rules such as scoring, movement, difficulty, timing, and win conditions. After creating or adapting a game, participants will play-test their result and answer short reflection questions about the choices they made.

- The study can be completed remotely on a compatible Apple device.
- No programming or prior game design experience is required.
- Participation is voluntary and can be stopped at any time.
